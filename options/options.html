Getting Started

Click the Play button on the Timer tab to start a focus session. You can choose between 25/5 (Pomodoro), 50/10 (Extended), or set a custom duration. The timer will run in the background even when the popup is closed.
Go to the Quick tab and use the "Add Quick Task" section. Enter a task title, select a priority level, and click the plus button. Tasks are automatically saved and will appear in your Dashboard.
Your streak represents consecutive days of studying. Complete at least one focus session per day to build your streak. Miss a day and your streak resets. Study consistently to earn badges and XP!

AI Features

Go to Settings, scroll to "AI Integration" section, and enter your Gemini API key. You can get a free key from Google AI Studio. Once configured, you can ask study questions in the Quick tab.
Your API key is stored locally in your browser using Chrome's secure storage. It is never sent to our servers. All AI requests go directly to Google's Gemini API from your browser.

Troubleshooting

Make sure browser notifications are enabled in Settings. Also check your browser's notification permissions for extensions. On Chrome, go to Settings > Privacy > Site Settings > Notifications.
All data is stored locally in your browser. Go to Settings > Data Management > Export Data to create a backup JSON file. Use Import Data to restore from a backup. We recommend exporting regularly.

Smart StudyOS

AI-Powered Study Productivity Suite

Our Mission

Smart StudyOS is designed to help students achieve their academic goals through intelligent study planning, focus management, and progress tracking. We believe that with the right tools and consistent effort, anyone can improve their learning outcomes.

Features

  • Pomodoro Timer - Focus sessions with customizable durations
  • AI Study Coach - Get personalized study advice powered by Gemini AI
  • Streak Tracking - Build consistency and earn badges
  • Task Management - Organize and prioritize your study tasks
  • Progress Analytics - Track your productivity over time
  • Privacy First - All data stored locally in your browser
